Built for the people who trade on this route

关于萨维利

Saweli started with a simple question from traders in Mogadishu: how do I know the supplier is real, the goods are right and my money is safe? Everything we do answers one part of that.

Our view我们的看法

Trade between East Africa and China is growing every year, yet most orders still run on WhatsApp messages and trust. We want every order on this route to be checked, documented and paid through a bank.

How we work工作原则

What we hold ourselves to

Show the cost

Every fee is in the quote before you confirm. No surprises at the port.

Check first

No money moves to a supplier we have not checked.

Speak your language

Somali, Swahili, English and Chinese, from the first message.

Write it down

Every step leaves a document under your order reference.

Banks hold the money

Payments go through partner banks. We do not take deposits.

Both sides matter

Exporters in East Africa get the same care as importers.

Where we are going发展方向

Somalia first, then Kenya and China

We start with trade into Somalia, where the need is greatest. Kenya and a presence in China come next, so that both ends of the route are covered by our own people.
