Know what you are paying for

银行合作

Banks in East Africa use Saweli to check the customer, the supplier and the documents behind a trade payment before it is released.

The problem问题所在

Trade payments with little behind them

A customer asks to pay a supplier in China. The invoice may be real, the supplier may exist, the goods may ship. Checking all of that from East Africa is slow and expensive.

Customer checks

Know your customer and know your business checks on the importer or exporter.

Supplier checks

The company in China is registered, real and matches the receiving account.

Goods checks

An inspection report confirms the goods match the invoice before release.

Document checks

Invoice, packing list and bill of lading checked against each other.

One record

Every check sits under one reference you can audit later.

Your workflow

Reports arrive in the format your trade finance team already uses.

How it fits合作方式

We check, you decide

Saweli does not hold funds or take deposits. We give the bank the evidence it needs, and the bank decides whether to release the payment.

  • The bank keeps the customer and the account
  • Saweli supplies the checks and the reports
  • Payment moves through the bank's own channels
  • Each payment is linked to an inspected shipment

Common questions

Does Saweli hold customer money?

No. Payments go through the partner bank. Saweli does not take deposits or hold funds.

Which checks do you run?

Customer verification, supplier registration and factory checks, inspection of the goods and a cross-check of the trade documents.

Can we see a sample report?

Yes. Ask us and we will send a sample with the customer details removed.

Which countries do you cover?

Somalia first, then Kenya. Suppliers anywhere in mainland China.
