The journey流程

From a factory in China
to your shop

01

Tell us what you need

Send the product, quantity and port, or the details of a supplier you already use. A person who speaks your language picks it up.

02

We source and quote

We find suppliers in Yiwu, Guangzhou and the factory towns around them, compare offers and send one quote with every cost shown.

03

We check the supplier

Business licence, factory and export history are checked before any money moves. Suppliers that fail are dropped.

04

Goods are inspected

Our inspector checks quantity, quality, packing and labels against your order and sends you the report with photos.

05

We ship to your port

Sea, air or road. We book the freight, prepare the documents and track the shipment to Mogadishu, Mombasa, Dar es Salaam or Djibouti.

06

Payment is settled

Payment goes through a partner bank with the invoice and inspection report attached, so every party sees the same record.

Questions常见问题

Good to know

Something else? Call us on +252 614 264 999

Is Saweli a bank?

No. Saweli arranges trade and verification. Payments are processed by our partner banks, and Saweli does not take deposits.

Can I use a supplier I already work with?

Yes. Send us the supplier details and we will verify the company and inspect your order in the same way.

How do I know the goods are right before they ship?

We inspect the goods against your order before they leave and send you the report with photos. You decide whether the shipment goes.

What does it cost?

It depends on the order value, the inspection and the freight. You see every fee in the quote before you confirm.

What is the smallest order you take?

We handle full containers, shared containers and air parcels. Tell us what you need and we will suggest the cheapest way to move it.

Which languages do you work in?

English, Somali, Swahili and Chinese.
