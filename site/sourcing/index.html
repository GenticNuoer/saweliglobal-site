Supplier checks供应商核查

What we check before you pay

A supplier that fails any of these checks does not get your order.

Registration

Business licence, registered name and the person who signs.

Factory

A visit or a live video walk through the production line.

Export history

Past shipments abroad and the markets they went to.

References

Other buyers who have ordered from the same supplier.

Samples

Product samples checked against your specification.

Bank details

The receiving account matches the registered company.
