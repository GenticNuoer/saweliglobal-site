Quick answers

Is Saweli a bank?

No. Saweli arranges trade and verification. Payments are processed by our partner banks, and Saweli does not take deposits.

Can I use a supplier I already work with?

Yes. Send us the supplier details and we will verify the company and inspect your order in the same way.

How do I know the goods are right before they ship?

We inspect the goods against your order before they leave and send you the report with photos. You decide whether the shipment goes.

What does it cost?

It depends on the order value, the inspection and the freight. You see every fee in the quote before you confirm.

What is the smallest order you take?

We handle full containers, shared containers and air parcels. Tell us what you need and we will suggest the cheapest way to move it.

Which languages do you work in?

English, Somali, Swahili and Chinese.

Trade between East Africa and China. Verified suppliers, inspected goods, freight to your port and payment through partner banks.
